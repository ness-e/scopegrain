# Methodology

For changes, inspect existing behavior, agree an observable outcome and
falsifiable requirements, obtain specification/design/plan approval, then
implement, verify, review and accept with traceability.
Scale artifacts to risk; read-only reporting does not need a new change spec.
Honor the project's selected runtime gates and state their actual limits.
Do not fabricate approvals, requirements, metrics or test evidence.
Manual/legacy operation is not automatic enforcement or a universal sandbox.
