# Compare Options

Read the captured research and the actual decision constraints.
Preserve frozen measurements and inputs; do not rerun derivations merely
to format or rank their results. Investigate new gaps as a separate action.

Compare viable alternatives, failure modes, reversibility, maintenance,
privacy, required permissions and supported environments.
State uncertain assumptions and evidence that could change the recommendation.
Do not invent scores, costs, licensing clearance or superiority.

Present a recommendation with the complete consequences and ask the owner
to decide where authority or product intent is required. Record the decision
bound to reviewed inputs; recommendation alone does not authorize execution.
