# Specify a Change

Inspect the repository, prior specifications and relevant behavior first.
Clarify the requested outcome, actors, non-goals, boundaries and constraints.
Separate facts, assumptions and consequential unknowns. Ask the owner about
product intent and authority; investigate answerable technical facts.

Write requirements with stable identifiers and observable scenarios,
including errors, cancellation, compatibility and unavailable capabilities.
Agree evidence and acceptance with the owner. Do not invent a baseline,
business metric, threshold, file, API or successful measurement.

Present alternatives and tradeoffs before recommending a design.
Record explicit specification approval bound to the reviewed content.
Store operational specifications in harness paths, not product docs.
Return the approved contract or unresolved questions; do not implement it.
