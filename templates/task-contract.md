Formal SDD additions only:
- Current specification and design identities/digests:
- Plan identity or single-task approval:
- Assigned requirement/scenario references (at least one per formal task):
- Current content-bound approvals:
- Bound result report and acceptance evidence:

Do not add the formal fields as empty ceremony to a light task.
Values are attributed statements, not authenticated identity or proof of
execution. Do not persist secrets; obtain an approved redacted request first.
